401(k) Retirement Plan Summary
ACME People · Plan year 2026

The ACME 401(k) plan helps you save for retirement with pre-tax (traditional) or after-tax (Roth) contributions, plus an employer match. Manage your election in ACME People → Benefits → Savings & protection.

Employer match

ACME matches 50% of your contributions, up to 6% of your eligible pay. That means if you contribute at least 6%, ACME adds an amount equal to 3% of your pay.

You contribute ACME matches Total going in
3% 1.5% 4.5%
6% (full match) 3.0% 9.0%
10% 3.0% (capped at 6%) 13.0%

Example: On a $120,000 salary, contributing 6% is $7,200/year; ACME adds $3,600/year. Contributing 10% is $12,000/year, but the match is still $3,600 (the match caps at 6%).

To get the full match, contribute at least 6%. Anything below 6% leaves free employer money on the table.

Vesting

Your own contributions are always 100% yours. Employer matching contributions are 100% vested immediately — there is no waiting period at ACME.

Contribution limits (2026)

  • Elective deferral limit: $23,500 per year.
  • Additional catch-up if age 50+: $7,500.
  • You may split contributions between traditional (pre-tax) and Roth (after-tax).

Changing your contribution

You can change your contribution percentage at any time — it is not limited to open enrollment. In ACME People, go to Benefits → Savings & protection, set your 401(k) contribution (%), and save. Changes take effect on the next pay run.
