2026 Benefits Guide
ACME People · Effective January 1, 2026 · Open enrollment ends June 30

This guide summarizes the health and welfare benefits ACME offers to eligible full-time employees. Elections are made in ACME People → Benefits. Coverage is effective the first of the month after your election (or January 1 for open-enrollment changes).

Medical plans

ACME offers three medical plans. Premiums below are the employee-only cost per bi-weekly paycheck (26 paychecks/year).

Plan Type Per paycheck Best for
ACME PPO Plus PPO $180.00 Most flexibility; see any provider, no referrals
ACME HMO HMO $120.00 Lower cost; in-network care with a primary doctor
ACME HDHP + HSA High-deductible $70.00 Lowest premium; pairs with a tax-advantaged HSA
Waive medical — $0.00 You have other qualifying coverage

PPO Plus vs. HMO vs. HDHP — how to choose

  • PPO Plus has the highest premium but the lowest deductible and lets you see out-of-network providers without a referral. Choose it if you want maximum choice.
  • HMO is mid-cost. You pick a primary care physician and stay in-network. Good balance of cost and simplicity.
  • HDHP + HSA has the lowest paycheck cost and a higher deductible. ACME contributes to your Health Savings Account, and your own HSA contributions are pre-tax. Best if you are generally healthy or want to save tax-free for medical costs.

Dental & vision

  • ACME Dental PPO — $14.00 per paycheck. Preventive cleanings covered 100%.
  • ACME Vision — $6.00 per paycheck. Annual eye exam plus a frames/contacts allowance.

Health Savings Account (HSA) & FSA

If you enroll in the HDHP, you can contribute to an HSA. For 2026 the IRS limits are $4,300 (individual) and $8,550 (family). ACME contributes $750 (individual) or $1,500 (family) to your HSA each year. A general-purpose FSA is available with the PPO and HMO plans.
