Payroll & Pay Guide
ACME People · 2026

Pay schedule

ACME pays on a bi-weekly schedule — 26 paychecks per year, every other Friday. If a payday falls on a holiday, pay is deposited the prior business day. Each pay period covers two prior weeks (for example, work from June 7–20 is paid on June 26).

Direct deposit

Set up or change direct deposit in ACME People → Payroll & Pay → Direct deposit.

  • Enter your bank name, routing number, account number, and account type (checking or savings).
  • You can deposit your entire net pay to one account, or split a partial amount across up to three accounts.
  • New or changed accounts go through a one-cycle prenote verification; your first affected paycheck may arrive as a paper check.

Tax withholding (Form W-4)

Federal income tax withholding follows the elections on your W-4, found under Payroll & Pay → Tax withholding. You can update it at any time.

Field What it does
Filing status Single / Married filing jointly / Head of household — sets the base withholding table
Multiple jobs Check if you or your spouse have more than one job, for more accurate withholding
Dependents amount Annual credit for qualifying dependents, which lowers withholding
Extra withholding An additional flat dollar amount withheld each paycheck
Changing to Married filing jointly generally lowers your federal withholding versus Single. Adding extra withholding increases the amount taken out each paycheck — useful to avoid a tax-time balance due.

Pay statements & W-2

Every pay statement is available in ACME People → Documents. Your annual W-2 is posted by January 31. Year-to-date gross, taxes, and deductions appear on each statement.

For administrators — running payroll

Payroll admins process each cycle under Payroll & Pay → Run payroll: select the pay period and pay group (e.g., US — Salaried or US — Hourly), optionally Preview the run, then Run payroll to post the pay date.
