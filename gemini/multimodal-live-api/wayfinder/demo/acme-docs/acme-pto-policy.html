Time Off & Leave Policy
ACME People · 2026

ACME provides paid time off so you can rest, recharge, and handle life. Balances and requests are managed in ACME People → Time Off.

Annual allowances (full-time)

Type Annual amount Accrual
Vacation (PTO) 20 days Accrues each pay period (~1.54 days / bi-weekly)
Sick 10 days Granted at start of year
Personal 2 days Granted at start of year

Employees with 5+ years of service accrue 25 vacation days per year.

Carryover

You may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next calendar year. Sick and personal days do not carry over. Unused carryover beyond 5 days is forfeited on December 31.

Requesting time off

  • Go to ACME People → Time Off → Request time off.
  • Choose the type (Vacation, Sick, Personal, or Unpaid), set start and end dates, and submit.
  • Requests route to your manager for approval; you'll be notified when approved or denied.
  • Submit vacation requests at least two weeks in advance when possible.
  • Sick time may be logged retroactively after an absence.
Negative balances: You cannot request more vacation than you have accrued. Check your available balance at the top of the Time Off page before submitting.

Company holidays (2026)

ACME observes 11 paid holidays, including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (two days), and the winter break (Dec 24–25, plus Dec 31). Holidays do not count against your PTO.

Other leave

Parental leave, bereavement, jury duty, and medical/FMLA leave are available per policy. Contact the People team to start a leave-of-absence request.
