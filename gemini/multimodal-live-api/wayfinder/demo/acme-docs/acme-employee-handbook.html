About ACME

ACME is an AI-first HR company. Our mission is to make work admin disappear so people can focus on their best work. We value clarity, ownership, and kindness.

Working at ACME

  • Hybrid by default. Most teams work in-office Tuesday–Thursday and remote Monday/Friday.
  • Core hours are 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. local time; outside that, manage your own schedule.
  • Equipment. ACME provides a laptop and a $500 home-office stipend in your first year.

Using ACME People (self-service)

ACME People is where you manage everything about your employment:

  • Personal Information — update your contact details, address, and emergency contact.
  • Payroll & Pay — direct deposit, tax withholding, and pay statements.
  • Benefits — medical, dental, vision, 401(k), HSA/FSA, and life insurance.
  • Time Off — check balances and request vacation, sick, or personal days.
  • Documents — W-2s, pay statements, the benefits guide, and this handbook.

Code of conduct (summary)

Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Protect confidential and personal data. Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose them promptly. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager or the People team.

Expenses

Submit business expenses within 30 days with a receipt. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle. Reasonable travel, meals, and team events are eligible per the expense policy.

Need help? Reach the People team at peopleops@acme.com or open the Ask Wayfinder assistant in ACME People for instant answers grounded in these policies.
